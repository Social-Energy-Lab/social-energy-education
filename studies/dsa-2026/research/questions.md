# Research questions: DSA 2026

From the PhD project (see `docs/context/theory.md`). This page records which questions this field phase can speak to, and with which data. Everything below the project's own RQs is a proposal until the research team confirms it: `[inferred]`.

## Project research questions

1. How can social energy in educational settings be grasped theoretically and empirically?
2. How does participation in circulating social energy affect young people's educational success?
3. How do institutional structures, pedagogical practices and interaction patterns shape the emergence, flow or blockage of social energy?
4. What particular barriers do young people with a migration background or from disadvantaged families face? (DSA is selective, so this needs the other settings.)
5. How can structures, practices and interaction patterns be adapted to support talent development for all?

## Guardrails

- Unit counts are small (~100 people). Prefer descriptive, visual and within-person analyses before inferential claims.
- Report only aggregates. Suppress small groups.
- Model eco-mode missingness and exclusion windows explicitly. Never interpret "no contacts" as "alone" without checking both.
