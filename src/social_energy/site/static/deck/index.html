The logic, in our words

Membranes, gradients and moments that matter

  • Trust in life and lust for life matter for social energy and for education alike.
  • Each is described by process variables (six survey scales each). Together they make up a person's membrane.
  • Where membranes differ there is a gradient: a potential for reaction and flow between people. A reaction may also be held back.
  • Moments that matter open or close a membrane. First person: a button press, the interviews. Third person, as a hypothesis: EDA and heart rate.
  • Context: beacons for the group, the programme for the institution, survey and interviews for the person.

Step 1 · bringing the data together

Full vs cleaned, per source

  • Every source is linked through one table per person and study ID, and filtered by consent per module.
  • Cleaning flags rather than deletes: duplicates, implausible times, restarted tags, records outside the camp, staff. Presses are merged into episodes.
  • The programme (timetable, special days) is in the study configuration, on the same clock.
  • Still to collect: the survey and the AI interview after the academy.
People are those the kept records cover. Survey: one duplicate submission removed; the confirmatory unit is people with beacons and a survey.

Step 2 · time sync

One clock for every source

  • Beacons: each tag's clock drifts; corrected per tag from its readouts. {beacon_reboots} tag restarts handled.
  • EDA wristbands: synced to a reference clock where one was recorded, otherwise by rate or by the charging box's co-motion. {eda_clock_uncertain_pct}% of worn minutes stay flagged as less certain; every EDA result is re-checked on the certain minutes ({eda_certain_wearers} wearers).
  • Interviews: recorded in local time, aligned to the camp clock.
  • Programme: in local time; everything is stored in UTC.

From any moment: who was close, which programme item, what EDA did.

Survey and the post-academy interview need no sync: before/after is enough. The uncertain EDA minutes are good to seconds or tens of seconds; minute-level results use all minutes and agree with the certain subset.

Step 3 · processing

What each source becomes

Survey

Scored to trust in life and lust for life, six scales each. Composites fixed before the confirmatory test.

Button presses

Presses become episodes; accidental chains are removed; self-report consent only.

Beacons

Close minutes per 5-minute bin, ties per day, per programme phase and setting.

EDA and heart rate

Skin-conductance responses, tonic level, heart rate and movement per minute. Worn in daytime only.

Interviews

Coded for the membrane with a draft codebook: model coded, a fifth checked by a second coder (agreement κw {membrane_kappa_now} on the state now).

Acoustics

Room loudness per hour. Not part of today.

Not yet: interviews coded by topic; written descriptions for self-reports (we do not have them).

confirmatory Results so far

The test fixed in advance: an honest null

  • Plan frozen before any survey scale was scored; code fixed before it touched the data.
  • Baseline trust and lust for life do not predict network strength, cross-course mixing or presses at the strength we could detect (n = {n_confirmatory}).
  • Moderate effects are not ruled out: the next phases repeat the tests and pool them.

exploratory Results so far

Moved moments are shared

  • Presses cluster among people who are physically together: {cascade_excess}× the pairs expected when each person keeps their own timing ({cascade_excess_lo} to {cascade_excess_hi}).
  • The excess peaks within seconds ({latency_0_10_ratio}× at 0–10 s) and grows with the group: a shared moment, not a chain.
  • It holds across weak ties too, not only among friends.

exploratory Results so far

Courses form circles; special days break them, briefly

  • On ordinary days {mixing_ordinary_lo}–{mixing_ordinary_hi}% of tie time crosses courses; random mixing would give {mixing_random}%.
  • Excursion {mixing_excursion}%, rotation day {mixing_rotation}%: the largest and most robust effect in the data.
  • But ties made on special days come back less often: {persistence_excursion}% vs {persistence_ordinary}% within three days.

exploratory Results so far

EDA: no peak at the button

  • {eda_wearers} wearers, daytime only; {eda_presses} presses by {eda_pressers} people fall in EDA time.
  • Around a press, skin conductance stays inside the shuffled-press band. The heart-rate rise at presses is movement.
  • Meals: skin conductance {+eda_meal_scr} SD, heart rate {+eda_meal_hr} SD against course blocks. Not one "arousal" axis.
  • Close pairs move together a little: r {eda_sync_close} vs {eda_sync_apart} apart.

So "EDA special moments" stay a hypothesis to test, not a definition.

The evening, where presses and shared moments concentrate, is not covered by EDA. SCR thresholds are ours and unvalidated; both rules agree.

  • {membrane_opened_pct}% describe their membrane opening over the academy; {membrane_stable_pct}% stable. {membrane_selective_pct}% call it selective: open to some, closed to others.

The membrane vocabulary was primed by a lecture the day before for a third of the interviews; the direction of the answers is still theirs.

exploratory Step 5 · the gradient theory

Do opposite membranes attract? A first test

  • {gradient_people} participants, {gradient_pairs} pairs. Course, joint time at the camp and each person's own sociability held fixed; the null reshuffles membranes within courses.
  • Opposites do not attract. Similar people spend slightly more time close, on trust in life only, mostly belonging: close minutes change by {gradient_trust_scales_pct}% per SD of difference ({gradient_trust_scales_within_pct}% within a course).
  • Pairs who rarely meet are not more different.
  • Shared presses: {gradient_excess_near}× chance timing for the most similar, {gradient_excess_far}× for the most different. The theory's direction, but not beyond reshuffling who carries which membrane: a lead.
Outside the frozen plan, no error control, many measures. Differences of noisy scores are noisier still, which pulls every effect towards zero. Fresh data is the test.

For the team

Gaps and questions

  1. Self-report descriptions. Some presses were meant to come with a written description. We never received them: where are they?
  2. Face to face. Beacons say how close, roughly, not whether people faced each other. Is proximity enough, or do we need another instrument?
  3. Interview topics. Coded for the membrane only. Which topics should we code?
  4. EDA moments. No peak at presses and no evening coverage. Keep them as a hypothesis?
  5. Confirmatory readings. Two choices in the frozen plan need your confirmation.

Proposal

Preregister the next phases

  • New confirmatory tests: shared moments, company at presses, special-day mixing and how long its ties last.
  • Replications: the three tests from DSA 2026 unchanged, pooled across phases.
  • Gradient theory: one distance measure fixed in advance (the trust profile, belonging), on fresh data, with EDA moments and interview context as secondary.
  • Instruments: EDA into the evening; the membrane question at fixed points (start, middle, end); daily beacon readouts; a timetable with end times.

Decide before START-Stiftung's data are read. Draft: next-phase preregistration.
